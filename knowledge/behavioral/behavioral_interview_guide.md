# Behavioral Interview Guide

Core Skills

- Communication
- Teamwork
- Conflict Resolution
- Problem Solving
- Adaptability
- Ownership
- Time Management
- Learning from Feedback

Interview Questions

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate. How did you solve it?
- Describe a time you had to explain something technical to a non-technical person.
- Tell me about a mistake you made. What did you do, and what did you learn?
- Describe a time you had to learn something new very fast.
- Tell me about a time you had too many tasks at once. How did you prioritise?

Junior Questions

- Tell me about a school or team project. What was your part, and what went well?
- How do you ask for help when you are stuck on a task?

Mid Questions

- Tell me about a time you helped a new colleague get started.
- Describe a time you pushed back on a deadline or a request. How did you handle it?

Senior Questions

- Tell me about a time you led a team through a difficult change.
- Describe a hard decision you made with incomplete information. What was the result?

Answer Method (STAR)

- Situation: where and when, in 1-2 sentences
- Task: what you had to do
- Action: what YOU did (say "I", not "we")
- Result: what happened, with a number if possible
